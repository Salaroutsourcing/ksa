Super Legalization Assistance

Clarify additional authentication requested after an earlier legalization stage.

The short answer

“Super legalization” can be used differently by different organisations. Ask who requires the additional stage and exactly which previous signature or document must be authenticated.

Who this is for

Applicants asked for another legalization or verification after obtaining a stamp or certificate.

How we help

We help you understand requirements, organise preparation and identify applicable submission options. Submission assistance is only offered where officially permitted.

A practical process

  1. Collect the written request for the extra stage.
  2. Identify who issued the existing authentication.
  3. Confirm the competent embassy or destination authority.
  4. Check whether originals, translations or a new submission are needed.

What to confirm before submission

An additional stamp is not automatically required. We help clarify the request, without guaranteeing acceptance or recommending unnecessary stages.
