Transparency

Who performs the official attestation?

Government authorities and other competent institutions perform official attestations, verifications, apostilles, and legalization decisions. We are an independent private service provider that assists customers with document preparation, requirements, submission routes, and related processing.

We are not MOFA, HEC, IBCC, NAVTTC, any embassy, consulate, or government department. We do not claim government affiliation. Official attestation decisions are made solely by the relevant authorities.

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

It depends on the authority and document type. MOFA generally allows blood relatives to submit on your behalf, with an authority letter required for non-relatives. HEC permits authorized persons for walk-in urgent services with a proper authority letter. IBCC permits only blood relatives. Requirements change — always confirm with the relevant authority before submission.
Yes, in most cases. University degrees and transcripts require HEC attestation before MOFA. Matric and Intermediate certificates require IBCC attestation. Personal documents like birth certificates or marriage certificates require NADRA or Union Council verification first. MOFA does not verify the underlying content — it authenticates the previous authority's seal.
It depends on your destination country. If the destination is a Hague Apostille Convention member (UAE, UK, Germany, France, Australia), an Apostille from Pakistan MOFA may be sufficient. If not (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait), you will need embassy or consular legalization after MOFA. Always confirm with the requesting authority in the destination country.
Do not alter documents. The correct route depends on which record is correct and which institution issued the document. For educational documents, contact your university or board about a correction or re-issue. An affidavit of "one and the same person" is sometimes accepted, but this varies. Get a document assessment before making changes. See our full guide: Degree Name Different From Passport.
HEC offers a designated courier service. IBCC offers courier submission (10–15 working days). MOFA allows courier for general documents. However, Power of Attorney, Divorce Certificate, Commercial Documents, and Affidavits generally require personal appearance or are processed differently. Requirements change — confirm with the relevant authority.
