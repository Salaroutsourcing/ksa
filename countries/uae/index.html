Destination guidance

Documents for the UAE

Start with the receiving organisation’s instructions for the UAE, then confirm the authentication, translation and delivery route that applies.

The short answer

For use in the UAE, establish what the receiving organisation needs and check UAE MOFA’s attestation instructions. Ask about any destination-side stage and translations before choosing a route.

Employment documents

Ask the employer or relevant professional body whether the request is for authentication, qualification recognition or both. These are different checks.

Family and personal records

Confirm whether the recipient asks for a marriage registration record, birth record or another specific certificate. Ask about accepted translations and document format.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Obtain the UAE recipient’s written requirements.
  2. Confirm the Pakistani issuer and any prerequisite authority stage.
  3. Check UAE MOFA’s current attestation instructions for the document.
  4. Confirm any UAE-side steps before arranging delivery.

What varies

The purpose, recipient, document format and translation requirements can change the route. No fixed fee, turnaround or acceptance is promised here.
