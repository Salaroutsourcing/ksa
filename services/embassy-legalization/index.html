Embassy Legalization Assistance

Make sense of consular stages and destination-specific requirements before paying for an authentication route you may not need.

The short answer

Ask the relevant embassy or consulate which process applies to the specific document and intended use. A route used for one destination or document may not apply to another.

Who this is for

Customers whose receiving authority asks for consular or embassy legalization.

How we help

We help you understand requirements, organise preparation and identify applicable submission options. Submission assistance is only offered where officially permitted.

A practical process

  1. Confirm the receiving institution and exact purpose.
  2. Check the relevant embassy’s current document instructions.
  3. Identify preceding verification and authentication stages.
  4. Confirm translation, appointment and any destination-side stage.

What to confirm before submission

Do not assume embassy legalization is always needed in addition to an apostille. Establish the applicable route before paying for either.
