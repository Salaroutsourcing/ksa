This page explains how this website handles information.

Browsing and preferences

This website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and how pages are used. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect information about your browser, device and interactions with the site. The hosting provider may also process connection logs. Theme and motion preferences are stored in your browser when available.

Analytics and enquiries

The site does not send enquiry message text or uploaded documents to Google Analytics through custom tracking. There is no document upload form. Google processes analytics information under its own privacy practices. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Case checker

The checker uses fixed choices in the current page. It has no upload field and does not automatically submit or store your answers. Reloading clears the selection.

Choosing a contact channel

A WhatsApp or email link opens an external service with the message you choose to prepare. That provider processes the information you send according to its own practices. Keep passwords, one-time codes and identity numbers out of the first enquiry.

Before sending documents

Ask who will access files, why they are required, how they will be transferred and the applicable retention and deletion arrangements. This site does not operate a document vault or promise a particular email retention period.

Questions and requests

Contact info@salaroutsourcing.com for privacy questions or access and deletion enquiries. Describe your request without attaching additional sensitive documents unnecessarily.
