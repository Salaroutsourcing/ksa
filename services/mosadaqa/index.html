Academic verification

Mosadaqa Guidance

Understand the verification request, prepare questions for your university, and check the official platform instructions.

The short answer

Mosadaqa is an academic certificate verification platform. Our role is independent preparation guidance; the platform and relevant institutions control verification and decisions.

Who this helps

Graduates who have received a request for academic verification for use in Saudi Arabia. Start with the exact wording of the request, not an assumed sequence.

What we can help you prepare

A record of the qualification, awarding institution, study details and any difference between your academic and identity records. We can help organise questions to ask the university or official platform.

What stays with you

You retain control of your account, consent, credentials and payments. Follow the official platform’s rules for applications. Never share a password or one-time code with an intermediary.

Before you begin

Confirm the current official checklist and whether your qualification is within scope. Ask the recipient whether it wants verification, recognition or another form of evidence; these are different decisions.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Clarify the recipient’s request
  2. Compare the academic and identity records
  3. Check current platform requirements
  4. Follow the official applicant route
