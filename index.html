    Preparation questions by destination

    UAE
    For use in the UAE, establish what the receiving organisation needs and check UAE MOFA’s attestation instructions. Ask about any destination-side stage and translations before choosing a route. UAE guidance ↗
    Saudi Arabia
    For Saudi Arabia, ask the requesting organisation whether the requirement is academic verification (often called mosadaqa), employment attestation or a trade-skills check. The stages differ, so keep the exact wording of the request and confirm whether an online verification submission, an authenticated original or both are expected. Saudi Arabia guidance ↗
    Qatar
    For Qatar, obtain the requesting organisation’s checklist and confirm the competent consular route. The document category, purpose and any translation request matter.
    Oman
    For Oman, check Convention applicability as well as the recipient’s requirements. An authentication route does not settle recognition or translation requirements.
    Bahrain
    For Bahrain, check the relevant treaty relationship and the document’s scope. Confirm with the receiving institution before arranging additional legalization.
    Kuwait
    For Kuwait, ask the relevant embassy and recipient about the applicable authentication stages. Check whether the request is for the original, a copy or a translation.
    UK
    For the UK, establish whether the recipient needs authentication, direct issuer verification or a different form of evidence. Confirm Convention applicability where an apostille is requested.
    Germany
    For Germany, review relevant Convention declarations and the German recipient’s instructions. Confirm translation and recognition requirements separately.
    France
    For France, ask the recipient which form of the document it accepts and whether a translation is needed. Verify the applicable authentication route before submission.
    Italy
    For Italy, establish whether the request concerns document authentication, academic recognition or both. Confirm the receiving institution’s requirements for translations.
    Canada
    For Canada, distinguish an apostille request from credential evaluation or direct academic verification. Check the particular receiving organisation’s instructions.
    Australia
    For Australia, ask whether the recipient needs an original, certified copy or issuer verification. Authentication and professional or academic recognition are separate checks.
    USA
    For the USA, identify the specific receiving institution and its document rules. An employer, university and licensing board may ask for different evidence.
    Europe
    Europe is not a single document jurisdiction. Identify the specific country and receiving organisation before checking the authentication route.
    Other
    Start with the exact destination country and receiving organisation. We can help identify which official instructions to check for your document and purpose.

    Good questions. Clear answers.

    A little clarity
    goes a long way.

    Are you a government authority?

    No. SK Attestations provides independent private assistance. HEC, IBCC, MOFA, embassies and other competent authorities make their own official decisions.

    Can you guarantee an attestation?

    No. Eligibility, approval and acceptance belong to the relevant authorities and receiving organisations. We can help you understand and prepare for the process.

    Can someone submit on my behalf?

    Representation, personal appearance and courier rules vary by authority and document. Check the current official instructions before authorising anyone or sending originals.

    Should I upload my documents here?

    No. This website has no document upload. Start with the document type, destination, purpose and a description of the issue. Do not send identity numbers or scans in the initial enquiry.

    Does an apostille work for every country?

    No. Check the Convention relationship, document scope and relevant declarations, as well as the receiving organisation’s requirements. Country membership alone is not a complete assessment.

    Do I need both an apostille and embassy legalization?

    Do not assume both are needed. Establish which route applies to the specific document and purpose, and confirm it with the competent authority.

    What does “mosadaqa” mean?

    Mosadaqa is used in Pakistan to mean the attestation, authentication or verification of a document, and most often a degree or certificate intended for use in Saudi Arabia. It is not a separate office: the document still has to be checked by its issuer and authenticated by the authorities competent for that document.

    Is mosadaqa the same as HEC attestation?

    No. HEC is one stage for eligible higher-education documents issued in Pakistan, and mosadaqa describes the authenticated or verified outcome the Saudi side expects. Which stages apply depends on your qualification, its issuer and the organisation receiving the document.

    What if my degree and passport names differ?

    Compare the records and contact the responsible issuer about correction or verification. Do not physically alter the document or assume an affidavit will be accepted.

    My degree, passport and CNIC names all differ. Where do I start?

    Compare the three records and write down the exact difference. Only the issuer of the incorrect record can change it: the university for the degree, DGIP for the passport and NADRA for the identity record. Resolve the difference before paying for attestation stages.

    Is an affidavit enough for a name difference?

    Do not assume so. Some organisations accept a sworn declaration or a registered name-change record, and others require the underlying record to be corrected. Ask the receiving organisation and the issuer in writing before paying for another stage.

    Are requirements the same for every destination?

    No. They vary by destination, receiving organisation, document and purpose. Obtain written instructions from the organisation that will use the document.

    Do you conduct NAVTTC tests?

    No. We provide independent test booking and technical document guidance. Official centres and programme authorities control eligibility, testing, availability and outcomes.
