MOFA Attestation Assistance

Understand document-specific prerequisites before the foreign affairs stage.

The short answer

Check the document category and prerequisite signatures before approaching MOFA. Do not assume that every document can be submitted in the same way.

Who this is for

People preparing Pakistani documents for a receiving organisation abroad.

How we help

We help you understand requirements, organise preparation and identify applicable submission options. Submission assistance is only offered where officially permitted.

A practical process

  1. Identify the original issuer and any prior authority stage.
  2. Check the latest MOFA instructions for this document category.
  3. Confirm identity, representation and appearance requirements.
  4. Confirm whether the destination needs an apostille or another legalization route.

What to confirm before submission

Fees, appointment arrangements, courier eligibility and personal appearance rules can change. Confirm them directly before payment or travel.
