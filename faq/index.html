Independent document guidance

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Understand our role, what varies and what to check first.

Are you an official authority or appointed agent?

No. We offer independent preparation and process guidance. We do not claim government affiliation, embassy appointment or authority to issue verification or attestation.

Can you complete an applicant-only step for me?

No. Where a process requires the applicant, direct consent, personal appearance or an officially permitted provider, that requirement must be followed.

Can you guarantee acceptance?

No. The relevant platform, issuing institution, embassy or recipient makes its own decision. Guidance does not guarantee approval, recognition or a visa outcome.

What should I send first?

Start with the document type, destination, purpose and a general description of the issue. Do not send scans, identity numbers, passwords or one-time codes in your first message.

Can you correct a name on my degree?

Only the responsible issuer can correct its official record. We help you compare the issue and identify questions for the issuer and receiving organisation.

Is QVP the same as Mosadaqa?

Do not assume the names refer to the same requirement. Confirm the exact programme and official instructions with the requesting organisation. Academic verification, employment-related checks and skills examinations are distinct questions.

Is a pending university reply a rejection?

Not necessarily. Read the official status or query and ask the relevant university office what response is needed before considering another application.

Does every UAE-bound document follow one route?

No. Check the official service and relevant mission instructions for the specific issuer and document, as well as the recipient’s accepted format.

Will the checker calculate an official price or completion date?

No. It gives preparation questions. Current official charges, private assistance fees and any timing estimate should be clarified separately for the actual case.

Does this website upload my documents?

No. The case checker works locally and accepts no files. Choosing a contact link opens WhatsApp or email; those providers process the information you choose to send.
