QVP and Mosadaqa: start with the right request

Different programme names do not automatically mean interchangeable requirements.

By SK Attestation ServicesPublished 2026-09-24Updated 2026-09-242 min read
The short answer

Do not choose a programme from its name alone. Clarify whether the request concerns an academic certificate, a profession-related qualification check or a practical skills examination.

Start with who is asking

An employer, professional body, education institution or processing authority may use different terminology. Ask for the named programme, official link and purpose in writing.

Academic certificate verification

For a Mosadaqa request, confirm the academic record to be verified and follow the current platform instructions. Verification is not a guarantee of recognition for every profession or purpose.

Employment-related qualification checks

Saudi professional-accreditation guidance addresses qualifications and their relationship to the profession. Confirm whether your occupation and case fall within the relevant programme’s scope.

A trade test is another question

A practical skills examination and verification of academic or experience records should not be treated as the same service. Ask which evidence or examination is actually requested.

Before opening an application

Have the qualification title, awarding institution, profession and recipient’s written instruction available. Keep personal account access private and verify the official route before payment.
