Your document, understood

Educational documents

Degrees, diplomas, transcripts and certificates each begin with the right issuer.

The short answer

Identify the qualification level and awarding body first. University, school and vocational records may follow different verification paths.

Start with the qualification

Write down the document title, awarding institution and level. A diploma alone does not identify an attesting authority.

What to check together

Compare names, programme titles, dates and institution details across the certificate and supporting academic record.

Your next step

Check HEC for eligible higher-education records, IBCC for relevant school qualifications, or the competent vocational authority for technical records.
