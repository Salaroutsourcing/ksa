Commercial and legal documents

Understand the document, the signatory and the intended use before arranging authentication for commercial or legal instruments.

The short answer

Identify whether the file is a corporate record, commercial instrument, agreement or power of attorney. Each can involve different issuing, witnessing and appearance requirements.

Document and signatory

Record who issued or signed the instrument, their capacity and the organisation requesting it. Avoid sending confidential commercial terms in an initial enquiry.

Confirm the competent route

Ask the relevant authority whether a chamber, notary, registrar or other institution has a role. Do not assume these are interchangeable.

Powers of attorney

Execution, identity verification and personal appearance can be material. Obtain instructions from the competent authority and seek qualified legal advice on the instrument’s legal effect.
