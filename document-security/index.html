Independent document guidance

Careful sharing starts here.

Your documents contain sensitive information. Begin with a description rather than a file.

What to tell us first

Document category, destination, purpose and the issue in general terms. Avoid full names, identity numbers, exact birth dates and private case references until there is a clear need.

What never belongs in a chat request

Do not share passwords, one-time codes or a request to bypass applicant consent. Use the official platform directly for steps that require your identity.

Before transferring files

Confirm the person handling the file, purpose, access, transfer method, retention and deletion arrangements. This website accepts no document uploads and does not claim a secure-storage service.

Correcting records

Changes to an official record must go through its issuer. Never erase, overwrite or digitally change an official document yourself.
