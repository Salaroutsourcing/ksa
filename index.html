Who Performs the Official Attestation?

Government authorities and other competent institutions perform official attestations, verifications, apostilles and legalization decisions. We are an independent private service provider that assists customers with document preparation, requirements, submission routes and related processing.

We are not MOFA, HEC, IBCC, NAVTTC, any embassy, consulate, or any government department. We do not claim government affiliation. Official attestation decisions are made solely by the relevant authorities.

MOFA generally does not attest educational documents that have not been verified by the relevant education authority first. Personal documents like birth certificates or marriage certificates require NADRA or Union Council verification before MOFA.
It depends on your destination country. If the destination is a Hague Apostille Convention member (e.g., UAE, UK, Germany, France, Australia), an Apostille from Pakistan MOFA may be sufficient. If the destination is not a member (e.g., Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait), you will need embassy or consular legalization after MOFA. Always confirm with the requesting authority in the destination country.

HEC offers a designated courier service. IBCC offers courier submission (processed within 10–15 working days). MOFA allows courier for general documents. However, certain documents — Power of Attorney, Divorce Certificate, Commercial Documents, and Affidavits — generally require personal appearance or are processed differently. MOFA general attestation is typically processed same-day (within 2 hours or before closing). HEC online scrutiny is followed by either a scheduled visit (same-day return) or courier (10 working days). IBCC appointment mode returns documents the same day; courier mode takes 10–15 working days; express service takes about an hour. These are indicative timelines — actual processing depends on case completeness and current authority workload.
