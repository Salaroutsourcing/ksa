Preparation questions by destination

UAE
For use in the UAE, establish what the receiving organisation needs and check UAE MOFA’s attestation instructions. Ask about any destination-side stage and translations before choosing a route. UAE guidance ↗
Saudi Arabia
For Saudi Arabia, ask the requesting organisation whether the requirement is academic verification (often called mosadaqa), employment attestation or a trade-skills check. The stages differ, so keep the exact wording of the request and confirm whether an online verification submission, an authenticated original or both are expected. Saudi Arabia guidance ↗
Qatar
For Qatar, obtain the requesting organisation’s checklist and confirm the competent consular route. The document category, purpose and any translation request matter.
Oman
For Oman, check Convention applicability as well as the recipient’s requirements. An authentication route does not settle recognition or translation requirements.
Bahrain
For Bahrain, check the relevant treaty relationship and the document’s scope. Confirm with the receiving institution before arranging additional legalization.
Kuwait
For Kuwait, ask the relevant embassy and recipient about the applicable authentication stages. Check whether the request is for the original, a copy or a translation.
UK
For the UK, establish whether the recipient needs authentication, direct issuer verification or a different form of evidence. Confirm Convention applicability where an apostille is requested.
Germany
For Germany, review relevant Convention declarations and the German recipient’s instructions. Confirm translation and recognition requirements separately.
France
For France, ask the recipient which form of the document it accepts and whether a translation is needed. Verify the applicable authentication route before submission.
Italy
For Italy, establish whether the request concerns document authentication, academic recognition or both. Confirm the receiving institution’s requirements for translations.
Canada
For Canada, distinguish an apostille request from credential evaluation or direct academic verification. Check the particular receiving organisation’s instructions.
Australia
For Australia, ask whether the recipient needs an original, certified copy or issuer verification. Authentication and professional or academic recognition are separate checks.
USA
For the USA, identify the specific receiving institution and its document rules. An employer, university and licensing board may ask for different evidence.
Europe
Europe is not a single document jurisdiction. Identify the specific country and receiving organisation before checking the authentication route.
Other
Start with the exact destination country and receiving organisation. We can help identify which official instructions to check for your document and purpose.
