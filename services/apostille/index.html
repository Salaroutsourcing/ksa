Apostille Assistance

Check whether the Convention route applies to your document and destination.

The short answer

An apostille route depends on the Convention being applicable between the relevant states, the document being within its scope and the receiving organisation’s requirements. Country membership alone is not the complete check.

Who this is for

Applicants whose receiving organisation requests an apostille on a Pakistani public document.

How we help

We help you understand requirements, organise preparation and identify applicable submission options. Submission assistance is only offered where officially permitted.

A practical process

  1. Obtain the receiving organisation’s written instructions.
  2. Check the HCCH status table, entry into force and relevant declarations or objections.
  3. Confirm the document category and prerequisites with the competent authority.
  4. Check translation and recognition requirements separately.

What to confirm before submission

An apostille authenticates origin; it does not establish that a qualification will be recognised or that the document’s contents will be accepted.
