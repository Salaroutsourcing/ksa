    Good questions. Clear answers.

    A little clarity
    goes a long way.

    Are you a government authority?

    No. SK Attestations provides independent private assistance. HEC, IBCC, MOFA, embassies and other competent authorities make their own official decisions.

    Can you guarantee an attestation?

    No. Eligibility, approval and acceptance belong to the relevant authorities and receiving organisations. We can help you understand and prepare for the process.

    Can someone submit on my behalf?

    Representation, personal appearance and courier rules vary by authority and document. Check the current official instructions before authorising anyone or sending originals.

    Should I upload my documents here?

    No. This website has no document upload. Start with the document type, destination, purpose and a description of the issue. Do not send identity numbers or scans in the initial enquiry.

    Does an apostille work for every country?

    No. Check the Convention relationship, document scope and relevant declarations, as well as the receiving organisation’s requirements. Country membership alone is not a complete assessment.

    Do I need both an apostille and embassy legalization?

    Do not assume both are needed. Establish which route applies to the specific document and purpose, and confirm it with the competent authority.

    What if my degree and passport names differ?

    Compare the records and contact the responsible issuer about correction or verification. Do not physically alter the document or assume an affidavit will be accepted.

    Are requirements the same for every destination?

    No. They vary by destination, receiving organisation, document and purpose. Obtain written instructions from the organisation that will use the document.

    Do you conduct NAVTTC tests?

    No. We provide independent test booking and technical document guidance. Official centres and programme authorities control eligibility, testing, availability and outcomes.

    Care starts with the first question

    Your documents contain
    sensitive information.

    Start with a description, not a document upload. The assessment runs in your browser and asks for no identity numbers. Before sharing files, clarify who will handle them, why they are needed and how long they will be kept.
