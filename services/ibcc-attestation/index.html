IBCC Attestation Assistance

Find the right starting point for school certificates, diplomas and board-issued records.

The short answer

The awarding body and qualification level matter. Check IBCC’s scope before treating a diploma or school certificate as a university qualification.

Who this is for

Applicants with school-level qualifications or a diploma whose attesting authority needs to be identified.

How we help

We help you understand requirements, organise preparation and identify applicable submission options. Submission assistance is only offered where officially permitted.

A practical process

  1. Identify the board or awarding body and the exact certificate title.
  2. Ask the issuer about verification and correction of record differences.
  3. Confirm IBCC eligibility, prerequisites and the permitted submission route.
  4. Check destination requirements after the educational stage.

What to confirm before submission

Not every document called a diploma follows the same route. Technical and vocational certificates may involve a different competent authority.
