UAE Embassy Requirements Guidance

Prepare for a UAE document request using the current official service and mission instructions.

The short answer

The official UAE service determines the applicable route based on the document and issuing authority. We help you understand the request and organise preparation questions, without claiming embassy authority.

Start with the receiving organisation

Ask whether the document is for an employer, education provider, family process or business use. Obtain its instructions before selecting a service.

Check the official channel

Use the official UAE document service and the relevant mission’s instructions to establish the applicable application route. Availability and document eligibility must be checked there.

Keep the scope clear

We do not perform official authentication, replace applicant consent or claim approved courier status. Restricted steps stay with the applicant or the officially permitted provider.

Document differences

Compare names, dates and document titles before proceeding. Ask the issuing institution about incorrect information and the recipient about any accepted translation.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Obtain the UAE recipient’s instructions
  2. Identify the document and issuer
  3. Check the current official application route
  4. Resolve record issues before submission
