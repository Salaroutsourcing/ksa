Questions we work through

01 / Identify

What document do you have?

Start with the exact document title, issuer, destination and purpose. Keep personal identifiers out of your first enquiry.

02 / Verify

Who issued it?

Compare the details against the issuer’s records. Ask the responsible institution about any mismatch before arranging authentication.

03 / Prepare

What comes first?

Confirm the current official checklist, accepted formats and submission arrangements. Keep originals with you until the route is clear.

04 / Attest

The relevant authority

The competent authority makes its decision. HEC, IBCC or another authority may be relevant depending on your document.

05 / Legalize

The applicable route

Confirm whether an apostille, embassy route or another process applies. Not every document needs every stage.

06 / Ready

For your next chapter

Check the completed document against the recipient’s instructions. Authentication does not guarantee recognition or acceptance.
