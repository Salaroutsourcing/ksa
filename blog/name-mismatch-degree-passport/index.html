Degree and passport names differ: what to check

Compare the records and identify the responsible issuer before making changes.

By SK Attestation ServicesPublished 2026-09-24Updated 2026-09-242 min read
The short answer

Identify which record is correct and which institution issued the record that needs attention. Never alter an official document yourself or assume an affidavit is a universal solution.

Make a simple comparison

Write the name exactly as it appears on the degree, passport and identity record. Note missing names, spelling differences and any formal name change. Keep this comparison private.

Ask the responsible issuer

A university answers questions about its academic record. The passport authority handles passport records. The identity authority handles identity records. One office cannot automatically correct another office’s document.

Ask the recipient what it accepts

The organisation receiving the document may ask for a corrected record, evidence of a name change or other supporting information. Get its answer in writing before purchasing additional services.

Avoid shortcuts

Do not erase, overwrite or digitally edit a name on an official document. A declaration does not itself change the source record, and its acceptance depends on the recipient.

Begin with a description

An initial enquiry can simply say which documents differ and what the difference is. Leave out passport numbers, identity numbers, birth dates and scans until the handling arrangements are clear.
